About

QZ Crafted Creations is a one person shop. I have collected Pokemon cards since the Base Set days and started 3D printing to build display pieces for my own binder. Friends wanted them too, so here we are. Every card is checked by hand, sleeved and shipped in a toploader. Sealed product is stored cool and dry and never resealed.
